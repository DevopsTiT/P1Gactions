What is Silva? What about SNOW?

Silva / Sylva:
- NOT a ServiceNow product or module
- Often means Sylva (telco/edge K8s framework) OR a person's name
- No required link to SNOW

SNOW:
- Short for ServiceNow
- ITSM tool that holds Incident tickets (INC0012345)
- In your workflow: Dynatrace Problem open → create SNOW INC; close → resolve INC

Your real pairing:
Dynatrace ↔ ServiceNow (SNOW) ↔ PagerDuty
Not Silva ↔ SNOW
