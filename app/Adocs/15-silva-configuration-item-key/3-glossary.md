| Term | What it means | Why you care |
|---|---|---|
| u_configuration_item | SILVA field for the affected host or component | Where the host CI goes |
| Operational status | Whether the CMDB record is in use | Non-Operational offerings may need review |
| Bracketed paste | Terminal wraps pasted text in `[200~ ... ~` | Causes "command not found"; retype or paste again |
| sys_class_name | Real table of a CI | Confirms cfbf255f... is a Service Offering |
