This search keeps the newest ACTIVEMQMODE for host wpalja307b. In this job that value is A.

Source on every row is ND_Mode_DC1. The line looks like ACTIVEMQMODE="A". The wider search had 1,486 events from 15 Nov 09:00 through 16 Nov 09:45. head 1 keeps only the newest one.

fetch logs, from: now()-24h
| filter contains(log.source, "ND_Mode_DC1", caseSensitive:false)
    and (
      contains(host.name, "wpalja307b", caseSensitive:false)
      or contains(content, "wpalja307b.prprivmgmt.intraxa", caseSensitive:false)
    )
| parse content, "LD 'ACTIVEMQMODE=\"' LD:ACTIVEMQMODE '\"'"
| filter isNotNull(ACTIVEMQMODE)
| sort timestamp desc
| limit 1
| fields timestamp, ACTIVEMQMODE
