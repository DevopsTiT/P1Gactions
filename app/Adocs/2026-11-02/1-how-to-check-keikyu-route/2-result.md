# Result

Take the local marked 品川. Leave the local marked 羽田空港. At 京急蒲田, take a train marked 品川, 泉岳寺, or 押上. Get off when the display says 泉岳寺.

The fare is the number on the ticket machine after you select 泉岳寺.
