# Glossary

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| 普通 | Local train. It stops at 六乡土手. |
| 品川 | Shinagawa. The right direction from 六乡土手. |
| 羽田空港 | Haneda Airport. The wrong train for 泉岳寺. |
| 京急蒲田 | The station where you change to a faster train. |
| 特急 | Limited express. A faster Keikyu train. |
| 快特 | A faster limited express. It still stops at 泉岳寺. |
| 押上 | Oshiage. Trains with this destination pass 泉岳寺. |
| IC card | A Suica or Pasmo. Tap in and tap out. The gate charges the fare. |
