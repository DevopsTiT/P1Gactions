You learn the ride from two places: a route search, then the station board.

1. Open 駅探, Yahoo 乗換, or Google Maps.
2. From: 六郷土手. To: 泉岳寺.
3. Read the train type, the change station, the minutes, and the sample fare.

At 六乡土手, read the board above the platform.

- 普通 and 品川: get on.
- 羽田空港: do not get on. That train goes to the airport after 京急蒲田.

At 京急蒲田, read the next board.

- Direction 品川, 泉岳寺, or 押上: get on.
- Stay on until the display inside the train says 泉岳寺.

Fare: on the Keikyu ticket machine, choose 泉岳寺. The price on that screen is today's fare. With Suica or Pasmo, tap in at 六乡土手 and tap out at 泉岳寺.

The 20 minutes and 280 yen were one search result from 2026-10-08. A clock time such as 19:50 was one line on a posted timetable. Check the search and the board on the day you go.
