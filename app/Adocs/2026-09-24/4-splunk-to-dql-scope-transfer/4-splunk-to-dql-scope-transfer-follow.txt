Splunk → Dynatrace: is scope different?

YES. That is why Splunk has many rows and Dynatrace has few.

SPLUNK
index="cs-digital-document-management" "error"
| search message="*error*" AND message!="*elivery not possible*"
= whole INDEX + error text + exclude

YOUR DQL
filter aws.log_group …/cs-digital-document-management-prod
= ONE prod Lambda log group only (narrower)

TEXT FILTERS
contains(content,"error") + not contains(…"elivery not possible")
= OK transfer of the message logic

BETTER TRANSFER
1) Discover:
fetch logs
| filter contains(aws.log_group, "cs-digital-document-management")
| summarize count=count(), by:{aws.log_group, log.source}

2) Wider (closer to index):
fetch logs
| filter contains(aws.log_group, "cs-digital-document-management")
| filter contains(content, "error", caseSensitive: false)
| filter not contains(content, "elivery not possible", caseSensitive: false)

3) Only then add prod if Splunk was prod-only too.

Also match the same time range in both UIs.
