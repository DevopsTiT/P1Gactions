Used clothes are a bad buy in a few cases. The category itself is a normal way to get clothes.

Skip used underwear, socks, and swimwear.
Skip a piece with a smell, stains, or mold that a wash will not remove.
Skip holes, a broken zipper, or stretched elastic.

A jacket, shirt, or pair of jeans you can wash is a normal purchase. Wash it before you wear it. Check the seams, the zipper, and the underarms in the shop.

Helmets and car seats are a different case. Those safety items have a service life. Shirts do not.
