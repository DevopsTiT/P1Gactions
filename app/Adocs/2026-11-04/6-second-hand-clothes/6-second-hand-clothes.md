# Second Hand Clothes

```
Is a used garment a bad buy?
  underwear, socks, or swimwear
    skip it
  smell, stains, or mold, and a wash will not fix it
    skip it
  holes, broken zipper, or stretched elastic
    skip it
  a jacket, shirt, or jeans you can wash
    a normal purchase
```

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Underwear, socks, or swimwear | Skip the used item. |
| Smell, stains, or mold that a wash will not remove | Skip the item. |
| Holes, a broken zipper, or stretched elastic | Skip the item. The garment is worn out. |
| A jacket, shirt, or jeans you can wash at home | Buy it if the size and fabric are right. |

## Summary

A used garment is a bad buy when it stays against your skin and cannot be cleaned well, or when the cloth is already worn out. A washed jacket, shirt, or pair of jeans from a second-hand shop is a normal purchase. Wash any used garment before you wear it.

## Investigation

| Check | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| The sentence "second hand clothes is bad" | It treats every used garment as the same object. |
| Skin-close items | Underwear, socks, and swimwear are the items people skip. |
| Condition | Smell, stains, mold, holes, and a dead zipper are properties of one item. |
| Outer clothes | Jackets, shirts, and jeans can be washed and worn. |

## Result

Wash a used garment before it touches your skin. Then use the table above on that one item.

A second-hand shop sells one garment at a time. Look at the seams, the zipper, and the underarms. If those are sound and the piece survives a wash, the purchase is ordinary. The price is often lower than a new piece of the same fabric.

Helmets and car seats are a separate case from shirts. Those safety items have a service life. Clothes do not work that way.
