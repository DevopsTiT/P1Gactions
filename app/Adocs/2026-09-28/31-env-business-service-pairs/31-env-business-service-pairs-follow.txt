Environment And Business Service Pairs

Each env has its own business service (uk-sap-fscd-dev, uk-sap-fscd-prd, ALJ_EIP_PRD).
The link table is service_offering: parent = business service, Environment = env.

Environment source order:
1. offering Environment (u_environment)
2. business service Used for (parent.used_for)
3. 3rd part of offering name: "uk-sap-fscd-dev - AXA GROUP OPERATIONS - Development - Standard"

Application = service name minus env suffix (-dev, -prd, _PRD, -tst ...).
