# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care |
|---|---|---|
| FIXED_ENVIRONMENT | Setting that forces the environment on every ticket. | Now Development. |
| DEFAULT_ENVIRONMENT | Fallback used only when FIXED_ENVIRONMENT is "" and no tag gives a value. | Also Development, so both paths agree. |
| Service offering | Specific offer record, one per environment here. | Should match the environment. |
| notFilled | Fields SNOW left blank after the POST. | Shows if the Development offering Name was wrong. |
