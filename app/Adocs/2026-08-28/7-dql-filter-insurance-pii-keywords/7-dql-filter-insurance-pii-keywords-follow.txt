DQL Filter Insurance PII Keywords — chat-ready follow

WHAT YOU HAVE
20 unique field names from Excel column E (insurance mail-service logs):
insuredPerson, subscriberGender, subscriberDOB, subscriberAddr, subscriberPh,
insuredGender, insuredDOB, insuredPersonKanjiName, insuredPersonKanaName,
contractPersonKanjiName, contractPersonKanaName, subscriberZipCode,
kanaFullAddress, kanjiFullAddress, displayName, loginId, lastName,
lastNameKana, dob, telNumberOld

Log formats: key: value, key=value, or JSON "key":

STEP 1 — COMBINED KEYWORD SWEEP (run first)
Paste in Logs → Advanced mode:

fetch logs, from: now()-24h
| filter in(dt.host_group.id, {
    "C_ALJ_BU_OS_A_INFRA_E_PRD_T_BASE",
    "C_ALJ_BU_BAP_A_HULFT_E_PRD_T_APP",
    "C_ALJ_BU_BAP_A_TAXPAYMENT_E_PRD_T_APP",
    "C_ALJ_BU_MIDDLEWARE-SHARED-PRODUCT_A_imageWARE"
  })
| filter matchesRegex(content, "(?i)(insuredPerson|subscriberGender|subscriberDOB|subscriberAddr|subscriberPh|insuredGender|insuredDOB|insuredPersonKanjiName|insuredPersonKanaName|contractPersonKanjiName|contractPersonKanaName|subscriberZipCode|kanaFullAddress|kanjiFullAddress|displayName|loginId|lastName|lastNameKana|\\bdob\\b|telNumberOld)")
| summarize hit_count = count(), by: { host.name, log.source }
| sort hit_count desc
| limit 50

STEP 2 — SAMPLE (after count > 0, limit 10 only)

fetch logs, from: now()-24h
| filter matchesRegex(content, "(?i)(insuredPersonKanjiName|displayName|loginId|telNumberOld)[=:\\s]+\\S+")
| fields timestamp, host.name, log.source, content
| limit 10

PER-CATEGORY QUICK FILTERS
Names Kanji: insuredPerson, insuredPersonKanjiName, contractPersonKanjiName, displayName, lastName, kanjiFullAddress
Names Kana: insuredPersonKanaName, contractPersonKanaName, lastNameKana, kanaFullAddress
Address: subscriberAddr, kanaFullAddress, kanjiFullAddress
DOB: subscriberDOB, insuredDOB, dob — pattern \d{4}/\d{1,2}/\d{1,2}
Phone: subscriberPh, telNumberOld — pattern 0[789]0\d{8}
Gender: subscriberGender, insuredGender
Zip: subscriberZipCode — 〒?\d{3}-?\d{4}
Login: loginId — email after key

MASKING (after discovery)
OneAgent: Settings → Log monitoring → Sensitive data masking
Example regex: (?i)(insuredPersonKanjiName|displayName|lastName)[=:\s]+[^,\n\}]+  →  $1=***

OpenPipeline replacePattern example:
fieldsAdd content = replacePattern(content, "(?i)(subscriberPh|telNumberOld)[=:\\s]+0[789]0\\d{8}", "$1=***")

VERIFY AFTER MASK (should return zero raw values)
fetch logs, from: now()-1h
| filter matchesRegex(content, "(?i)insuredPersonKanjiName[=:\\s]+[\\u4E00-\\u9FFF]")
| limit 10
