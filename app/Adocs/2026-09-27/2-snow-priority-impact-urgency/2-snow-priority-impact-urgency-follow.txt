ServiceNow priority = impact x urgency

Impact: how big (1 high, 3 low)
Urgency: how fast (1 high, 3 low)

Default matrix:
I1U1 P1 | I1U2 P2 | I1U3 P3
I2U1 P2 | I2U2 P3 | I2U3 P4
I3U1 P3 | I3U2 P4 | I3U3 P5

Out of box 5 levels; many companies show only P1-P4.
Do not send priority in API - SNOW recalculates. Send impact + urgency.

Our workflow:
CRITICAL/AVAILABILITY -> 1/1 -> P1
ERROR -> 2/2 -> P3
other -> 3/3 -> P5 (or P4)
