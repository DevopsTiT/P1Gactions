Enriched INC to SILVA

FLOW
Problem -> enrich-problem -> cmdb-lookup (retired=skip) -> post-silva-enriched-incident -> trigger-pagerduty

ENRICH SOURCES
Problems API: root cause, impacted, evidence, zones, tags, start time
Tags: app, env, owner, snow-group, runbook
DQL logs: top 3 errors last 30 min
DQL events: deployments last 2 h
Rules: impact from impactLevel+env, urgency from severity (non-prod = 3)

SNOW FIELDS
short_description "[Dynatrace][prod] <title> on <root cause>"
description = facts; work_notes = logs + deploys + sync keys
correlation_id, contact_type=monitoring, category, subcategory, impact, urgency
cmdb_ci, assignment_group (CI support group or snow-group tag), business_service
u_host, u_environment, u_application, u_dynatrace_problem_url (confirm exist)

SETUP
allowlist silvastg.service-now.com + events.pagerduty.com
credential vault IDs: __SNOW_CREDENTIAL_ID__, __PD_CREDENTIAL_ID__
tag entities; confirm u_ fields with SILVA; classic ITSM toggle off
