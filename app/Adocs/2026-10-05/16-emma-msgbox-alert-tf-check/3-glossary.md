# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Emma | Emma Life app; MsgBox is its message box feature |
| `caseSensitive: false` | Makes DQL `contains` ignore upper and lower case, like Splunk |
| `arrayMovingSum(x, 5)` | Adds each point to the 4 before it, giving a rolling 5-minute total |
| Teams channel email | Address that posts emails into a Microsoft Teams channel |
| Noise check | Running the alert query over history to see how often it would fire |
| Dealerting samples | Minutes under threshold before the problem closes |
