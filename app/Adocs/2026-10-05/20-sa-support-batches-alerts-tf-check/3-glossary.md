# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Absence alert | Fires when something expected did not happen (a batch did not log its lines) |
| `LESS_THAN 2` | Splunk condition: fire when fewer than 2 results were found |
| Heartbeat | A regular log line or signal that proves a job is alive |
| `1-5` (cron day of week) | Monday to Friday |
| `else = "SKIP"` | Workflow skips the task when its condition is false |
| Over-broad match | A filter that matches unrelated lines and hides the real signal |
