  2 nameLIKE dbType ^ nameLIKE envTag
  3 nameLIKE dbType ^ nameLIKE regionPrefix
  4 nameLIKE trigram
  5 cmdb_ci_service_technical nameLIKE dbType ^ nameLIKE envTag
  score: group 3, dbType 2, env 2, region 1, trigram 2, operational 1, business class 1, +1 per extra search
  pick best if >= MIN_SCORE 5 and ahead of 2nd; else top 10 in lookup.service_candidates
D offering by env; E group check
