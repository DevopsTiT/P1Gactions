Workflow Output Format — Complete Summary

7 blocks in display-result:
1 dynatrace_alert        what / where / how severe (Dynatrace)
2 servicenow_enrichment  owning business service (SILVA) — same keys as correctoutput.sh
3 decision               create_incident, reason, assignment_group(+id, from), environment
4 snow_incident_payload  body OPEN POSTs to SILVA
5 pagerduty_payload      body OPEN sends to PagerDuty
6 tags                   count, ago, other, raw
7 lookup                 found, method, search_terms, service_candidates, cis_found, steps

dynatrace_alert: service_name, alerting_profile, severity, event_name, error_rate,
problem_id, problem_url, impact_level, host, entity_id, entity_type, environment, environment_tag,
db_type, trigram, platform, region, maintenance

servicenow_enrichment: found, match_method, sys_id, business_service, number, category,
service_classification, assignment_group(+_id), support_group(+_id), company(+_id), managed_by,
u_bbsa_id, u_business_range, business_criticality, u_cmdb_properties, comments,
service_offering(+_id), configuration_item(+_id, _class, _fqdn), ci_support_group(+_id)

SNOW payload: short_description, description (+Additional Information JSON), correlation_id=problem_id,
impact/urgency 4 - Low, contact_type Event, u_environment, u_host, assignment_group id,
business_service / service_offering / cmdb_ci / company ids (only when found)

PD payload: routing_key placeholder, trigger, dedup_key dt-problem-<id>, summary, source host,
severity (Infrastructure=error else warning), group env, component trigram, custom_details

Group rule: tag group (in SILVA) > service support group > CI support group > default
Maintenance True -> create_incident false (SKIP_WHEN_MAINTENANCE)
