V5 default set fallback

New file: 8-v5-default-qa-platforms-fallback.workflow.yaml (v4 unchanged)

When BOTH business service and assignment group are not found:
Environment = PoC / VoA / Demo
Business service = QA Platforms
Service Offering = QA Platforms - AXA GROUP OPERATIONS (record name starts with this)
Assignment group = Ops_Middleware_Monitoring_AXAJP
Company = QA Platforms company, else AXA GROUP OPERATIONS
Assigned to = optional (DEFAULT_SET_ASSIGNED_TO, empty by default)
Impact = 4, Urgency = 4

Only service missing: business service = QA Platforms.
Only group missing: service group, CI group, then Ops_Middleware_Monitoring_AXAJP.

Output flag: snow_required.default_set_used = true.

The Oracle event will NOT use the default set because Database_AXAJP comes from the tag.
To test: remove group tags and host tag from SAMPLE_EVENT and press Run.

Do not push app/Adocs (password in YAML).
