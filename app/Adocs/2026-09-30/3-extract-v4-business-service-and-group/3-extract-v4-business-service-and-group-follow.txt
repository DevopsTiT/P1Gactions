Business Service And Assignment Group Workflow (v4, read only)

File: 3-extract-v4-business-service-and-group.workflow.yaml

What changed from step 1:
1. Every group tag is a candidate. The specific tag (AGO_ORACLE_ASSIGNMENT_GROUP) is tried before AGO_DEFAULT_ASSIGNMENT_GROUP.
2. Each group candidate is checked in SILVA (sys_user_group, active=true). The first real one wins, with its sys_id.
3. Business service order: SERVICE_MAP or service tag, then host or DB CI, then scored searches.
4. Search 1 uses the group sys_id exactly.
5. The environment comes from real environment tags first. The patch environment tag is only a fallback.
6. Maintenance comes from the event field first, then the tag.
7. The output starts with snow_required, ready_for_snow and missing.
