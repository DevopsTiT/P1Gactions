# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Alert Status Manager | Custom Splunk alert action that sends the alert to email and PagerDuty |
| PagerDuty Notification | Switch in Alert Status Manager; Enable means the alert pages on-call |
| pagerduty.enabled | Dynatrace event property read by the standard flow to decide whether to page |
| Standard flow | Dynatrace workflow that previews and then posts to SILVA and PagerDuty |
