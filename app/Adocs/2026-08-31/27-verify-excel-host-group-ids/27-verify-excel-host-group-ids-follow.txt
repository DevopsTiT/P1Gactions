VERIFY EXCEL COLUMN L HOST GROUP IDs

Dynatrace = source of truth.
Excel often wrong: spaces, ALJ vs ALI, DATAENGLF vs DATAENGI.

Live MDM (proved): C_ALI_BU_DATAENGI_A_CUSTMDMGM_E_PRD_T_APP

V1 list live:
fetch logs, from: now()-24h
| filter isNotNull(dt.host_group.id)
| summarize log_count = count(), by: { dt.host_group.id }
| sort log_count desc
| limit 200

| sort log_count desc
