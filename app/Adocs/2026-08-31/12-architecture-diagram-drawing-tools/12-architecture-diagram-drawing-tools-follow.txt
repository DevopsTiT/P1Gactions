DECISION

FOR YOUR SPLUNK→DYNATRACE ARB
1) Sketch in Excalidraw (optional)
2) Clean redraw in https://app.diagrams.net  (AS-IS, TO-BE, HLD)
3) Export PNG
4) Paste into the ARB PowerPoint
5) Keep the .drawio file as the editable source
