# Multilogforwarder Life Only Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| multilogforwarder | The log forwarder whose Dynatrace handler sends AXA Life Kubernetes logs into Dynatrace. |
| Log forwarder | A service that reads logs from storage and pushes them to a monitoring tool. |
| Namespace | A Kubernetes grouping for one app's pods. |
| li-jp | AXA Life Insurance Japan. |
| gi-jp | AXA General Insurance Japan. |
| Onboarding | Configuring an app so its logs reach Dynatrace. |
