# Why 80 DQL Simple Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Request ID | UUID shared by all log lines of one request. |
| Subsearch | Splunk inner search pasted into the main search. |
| `format` | Splunk command that builds an OR list of words. |
| `lookup` | DQL join of two queries on a shared field. |
| `parse` | DQL extraction of a value from log text. |
