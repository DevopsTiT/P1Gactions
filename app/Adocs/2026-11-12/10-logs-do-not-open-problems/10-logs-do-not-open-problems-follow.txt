The 514 lines prove the logs arrived. They do not open a problem.

Every visible line is INFO. The text is INIT_START Runtime Version. Lambda writes that line on a normal start. The screen is a Notebook search. A search lists logs. It does not write to Problems.

A problem opens when a saved alert condition is met. These startup lines do not meet an error condition.

Use a saved Timeseries alert for the error lines:

fetch logs
| filter contains(aws.log.group, "/aws/lambda/light-digital-api-dev-confirm-e-signature", caseSensitive:false)
| filter loglevel == "ERROR"
| makeTimeseries error_count = count(), interval:1m

Threshold above 0. Press Save.
A minute of INIT_START lines stays quiet.
A minute with an ERROR count above 0 opens the problem.
