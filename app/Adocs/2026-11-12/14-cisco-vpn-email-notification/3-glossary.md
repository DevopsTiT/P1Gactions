# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Email notification | The Dynatrace resource that sends mail when the profile matches. |
| profile | The link from the mail to the ALJ_VPN alerting profile. |
| to | The recipient list. It is still hidden on the Splunk form. |
| notify_closed_problems | A second mail when the problem closes. |
| {ProblemDetailsHTML} | The problem body Dynatrace fills in. |
