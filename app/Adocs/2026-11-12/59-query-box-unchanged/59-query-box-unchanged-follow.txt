   Condition: Below 101
   Window: a few minutes

   Title: TESTDUMMY host entity
   Event name: TESTDUMMY host entity
   event.type: CUSTOM_ALERT
   dt.source_entity: {dt.entity.host}
7. Leave Actor as shuge kui. Leave offset and delay empty. Leave Data type on Timeseries.
8. Click Save.
9. Wait about 3 minutes.
10. Open Problems and search TESTDUMMY.
11. Disable the alert after the problem shows.
