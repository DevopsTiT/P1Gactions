# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Timeseries | A count for each minute. The alert compares that count with a threshold. |
| Records | Individual log lines. A matching line is the alert. |
| Identity field | The stable field that keeps one open problem per function. |
| makeTimeseries | The command that turns log lines into a count per minute. |
| interval:1m | The one-minute bucket Dynatrace expects on a timeseries alert. |
