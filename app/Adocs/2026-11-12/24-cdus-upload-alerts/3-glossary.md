# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| pending | Started, and not completed, and not failed. |
| dedup | One row for each action and document before the count. |
| Greater than 5 | Six or more pending documents. |
| Greater than 1 | Two or more matching documents or lines. |
| ALJ_CDUS | The shared title prefix for these five alerts. |
| 8 hours | The Splunk suppress time on the batch alert only. |
