# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| 10 6 * * * | 06:10 every day. |
| Last 24 hours | The search window on this form. |
| Throttle 60 minutes | Splunk will not fire this alert again for 60 minutes. |
| ALJ_HPM | The title prefix for this detector, profile, and email. |
| index | The Splunk index. It stays in the query so ERROR stays in this API. |
