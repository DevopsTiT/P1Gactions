# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| sa-support-batches | The Splunk index on all three searches. |
| Less than 2 | The alert fires when zero lines match, and when one line matches. |
| ALJ_SABATCH | The prefix for the error and the timeout. |
| ALJ_ANNUITY | The prefix for the file-import mailbox. |
| 30 8 * * 1-5 | 08:30 on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday. |
| started | A short word in the file-import search. Inside this index it can match ordinary lines. |
