PagerDuty AI Architecture — zone picture

File: 26-pagerduty-ai-zone-architecture.pptx

Read it like the APM architecture page: three zones, a label on every line, one yellow note.
This picture is the war-room assist. It is not the APM or MultiSoft platform.

ZONE 1 — Signal sources
Dynatrace. Splunk. Selenium / Jenkins.
Network and VM: VeloCloud, Nagios, SCOM.
Cloud tools: CloudWatch, X-Ray, Prometheus.
Each tool keeps its current owner.
Line into PagerDuty: HTTPS event.

ZONE 2 — PagerDuty cloud
Incident and escalation policy.
Call and SMS.
Sign-in is Microsoft Entra ID with SAML 2.0 and MFA. The IdP is outside this cloud.
PagerDuty AI Assistant is the new box.
It writes four objects: summary, causes, status, Scribe.
Transit is TLS 1.2 or higher. At rest is AES-256.
PagerDuty states it does not train on customer data.
Confirm the service region with the account owner.
Shift schedule data is not sent.

ZONE 3 — War room
Microsoft Teams channel monitoring-2026-prod.
Primary, the on-call employee, asks and then checks the reply.
BAM-India receives the call and SMS, then escalates.
Technology SREs and Application Support work in the channel.
A late joiner reads the reply, then opens DEM, Nagios, SCOM, or the AWS tool.

Lines: notify, call, prompt.

BOTTOM
ServiceNow / Silva: correlation, filed by Primary after the fact-check.
Final PIR: Primary replaces the root-cause draft with confirmed evidence, then publishes.
If the assistant is down, the incident is still created and the escalation policy still runs.

Red dot is Primary. Customer, financial, and health fields are not inputs on this picture.
