SLIDE 2 — Data Flow
Use case: 24x7 critical-incident catch-up in Microsoft Teams. ALJ and ADJ (P&C).

1 Detector — service name, alert text, problem id.
2 PagerDuty — incident fields and the runbook. Red dot: assignee display name.
3 Teams — the question and one technical line. Red dot: who asks.
4 PagerDuty AI — SRE catch-up, Scribe note, Insights if enabled.
5 Text reply — catch-up, status draft, or bridge note. Red dot if a spoken name is repeated.
6 Person — checks the text, acts in PagerDuty, files the correlation.

Call and SMS stay inside PagerDuty. The on-call number is not copied into the prompt. Shift has no arrow.

Left off the picture: customer date of birth, My Number, financial fields, health fields, and the on-call schedule.
