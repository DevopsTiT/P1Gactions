# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care on this slide |
|---|---|---|
| User of the AI system | A person who types the question in Microsoft Teams. | Section 7, first block. |
| Consumer of the output | A person who reads the reply or the status and may not type the question. | Section 7, second block. |
| Support Function | The line-of-business choice for a team that supports the company rather than selling a product. | The tick for Technology DevOps. |
| Technology DevOps | The owner of PagerDuty, Dynatrace, and Splunk on the ALJ tool sheet. | The department that asks. |
| Application Support | The other group drawn in the war room. | They share the Teams channel. |
| BAM-India | The 24x7 desk that receives the notification on the monitoring diagram. | They read and escalate. They are consumers unless the pilot also links them as askers. |
| Acceptance criteria | The checks that must pass before the tool is accepted. | Section 8. Written for a test service. |
| Test service | A PagerDuty service that pages only the pilot, not the production rota. | How you run the five checks safely. |
| Usable reply | A reviewer can match the incident id and the open or acknowledged state to PagerDuty. | The pass mark. There is no percentage on this form. |
| Allowable downtime | How long the business can go on without this AI reply. | Section 9. Four hours, and only for the assist. |
| Escalation policy | The rule for who is called next if the first person does not acknowledge. | Stays on PagerDuty even when the assist is down. |
