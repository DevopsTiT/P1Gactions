Late joiners read the same Teams reply, then open the one tool their team owns.
- DBA — DEM (Shinagawa DC)
- Network — Nagios, VeloCloud, or Aruba UXI
- Axa GO — SCOM
- Cloud Broker — CloudWatch, X-Ray, Prometheus, or Kibana

ROLES
- Technology DevOps owns the detectors that feed PagerDuty, and the PagerDuty account.
- BAM-India receives the 24x7 call and SMS, then escalates.
- Technology SREs and Application Support ask in Teams, check the reply, and work the incident.
- A person sends any status. Scribe writes the bridge note. A person keeps that note.

PILOT BOUNDARY
- In: SRE catch-up and Scribe notes, on a test service.
- Insights: one question, only if that agent is enabled.
- Shift: no output. Schedule data is not sent.
- If the assist is down, the incident is still created and the escalation policy still runs.
- 4 hours is the downtime figure for the Teams assist only. Call, SMS, and escalation keep current PagerDuty availability.
