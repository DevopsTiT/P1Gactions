# Glossary

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Proposed business architecture | Who does what, and which system sits on which step. This slide is the business picture. |
| War room | The Teams channel where Technology SREs and Application Support work one incident together. |
| PagerDuty AI | The assist in that channel. Also labeled Advance. It writes text after the incident exists. |
| SRE agent | The catch-up reply for one open incident. Product name Paige. |
| Scribe | Turns bridge audio into a text note in the channel. |
| Insights | One answer from past incidents the user can see. Only if enabled. |
| Shift | On-call schedule helper. It has no output in this pilot. |
| BAM-India | The 24x7 desk that receives the call and SMS. |
| Silva | The correlation store used with ServiceNow after the war room has the story. |
| To Be | The label on the current 24x7 monitoring diagram. This slide adds Teams AI onto that path. |
