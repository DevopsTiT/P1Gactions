# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care on this slide |
|---|---|---|
| SSO | One company login opens PagerDuty. No separate password. | Question 1. |
| SAML | The standard PagerDuty uses for that company login. | How SSO is turned on. |
| MFA | A second check, such as a phone prompt, before login succeeds. | Done at the company identity provider. |
| Identity provider | The company login system. Here, Microsoft Entra ID. | Where MFA is switched on. |
| TLS | Encryption of data while it moves over the network. | Question 3. |
| AES-256 | Encryption of data while it sits on disk. | Question 4. |
| Service region | The country or area of the PagerDuty data center for this account. | Confirm it with the account owner. |
| AI Disclosure | PagerDuty’s document that names the model providers. | Attach it because question 2 is Yes. |
