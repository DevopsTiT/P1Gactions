Data Flow detail — two PowerPoint pages
File: 23-pagerduty-ai-data-flow-detail.pptx

PAGE 1 — eight hops

1 Detector to PagerDuty
Dynatrace, Splunk, Selenium/Jenkins, or a network tool sends the critical event.
Fields: service name, alert text, problem id.
No red dot.

2 Page to BAM-India
PagerDuty creates the incident, then places the call and SMS under the escalation policy.
The on-call number stays in the notification. It is not prompt text.

3 PagerDuty to the assist
Reads id, title, priority, status, service, timeline, and the linked runbook.
Red dot: assignee display name.

4 Person to Teams
One question. Optional one technical line from Dynatrace or Splunk.
The full log store is not connected.
Red dot: Teams identity of the person who asks.

5 Bridge to Scribe
Spoken words in. A text note in the channel out.
Red dot: an employee name spoken on the bridge.

6 Insights, if enabled
One question plus past incidents this user can see.
Red dot: the asker’s Teams identity.

7 Assist back to Teams
SRE catch-up, status draft, or bridge note.
A person sends any status.
Red dot if the note repeats an employee name.

8 Person, then ServiceNow / Silva
The person checks the text, acts in PagerDuty, and files the correlation.
The assist does not write this hop.

Shift is not a hop. Customer date of birth, My Number, financial fields, and health fields are not on this flow.

PAGE 2 — each agent

SRE reads the open incident, the linked runbook, the Teams question, and at most one technical line. It writes a catch-up.
Scribe reads bridge audio. It writes a text note.
Insights, only if enabled, reads history the user can see and writes one sentence.
Shift is not sent and has no output.
