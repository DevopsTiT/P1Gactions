SLIDE 19 — NOTES PANE

This slide is the business picture for the 24x7 war room.
Read it the same way you read the EIP architecture page: zones as boxes, one hub in the center, and a label on every line.

The detection zone on the left provides the signal. Dynatrace, Splunk, Selenium, and the network, VM, and cloud tools stay there. Each tool keeps its current owner.

PagerDuty is the hub. It already creates the incident, places the call and SMS, and runs the escalation policy. Technology DevOps owns that hub.

The call and SMS go to BAM-India. They escalate into the war room.

The war room is Microsoft Teams. Technology SREs and Application Support consume the briefing. A late joiner from DBA, Network, Axa GO, or Cloud Broker reads that same briefing, then opens the one tool their team owns.

PagerDuty AI is the thicker box inside Teams. That is the only new box. It writes a catch-up, a status draft, or a bridge note. A person checks the text, then acts back in PagerDuty.

ServiceNow and Silva stay at the bottom. Correlation happens after the person has acted.

Yellow note: Shift is not drawn, because schedule data is not sent. If the assist is down, the incident is still created and the escalation policy still runs.

If asked "why is the AI inside Teams?": the pilot reads and writes in the war-room channel.
If asked "is this the EIP mediator picture?": this slide uses that layout. The mediators stay on the EIP page.

SLIDE 18 — NOTES PANE

This slide is the same path, drawn as data.
Use case: 24x7 critical-incident catch-up in Microsoft Teams, for ALJ and ADJ (P&C), after PagerDuty has opened the incident.

Detector hop: service name, alert text, and the problem id when the monitor put it on the event.

PagerDuty hop: incident id, title, priority, status, service, timeline, and the linked runbook.
Red dot: assignee display name.

The on-call number stays in the PagerDuty notification. It is not copied into the prompt.

Teams hop: the question, and at most one technical line.
Red dot: Teams identity of the person who asks.

Scribe hop: spoken words on the bridge. The kept result is a text note.
Red dot: any employee name spoken on the bridge. Keep the dot if the note repeats that name.

Insights: only if enabled. Past incidents this user can see, plus one question.

Shift: no arrow. The schedule is not sent.

A person checks the text before they acknowledge, escalate, or send a status.
ServiceNow and Silva are filed by that person.

Customer date of birth, My Number, financial fields, and health fields are not on this picture.

THREE RED DOTS
1. Assignee display name on the incident.
2. Teams identity of the person who asks.
3. Employee name spoken on the bridge, and on the note if the name is repeated.
