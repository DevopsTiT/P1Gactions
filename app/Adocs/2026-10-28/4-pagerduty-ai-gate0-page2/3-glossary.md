# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care on this slide |
|---|---|---|
| Gate 0 | The early form that defines the problem and the capability before a build. | This is page 2 of 4 of the business requirements. |
| Capability | The kind of AI job the form can route. | Tick the closest boxes. The explain line carries the real job. |
| Document understanding | Reading large sets of documents, often with OCR, and pulling fields out. | The banner says this use case is not that job. |
| Other workplace assistants | The box for an assistant that helps an employee do their own work. | This is the right tick for an on-call assist. |
| PagerDuty AI | The assist that answers in Microsoft Teams after a PagerDuty incident exists. | It drafts. A person confirms. |
| Catch-up | A short briefing of what the incident is, what is already known, and what to check next. | The main gain in the value box. |
| Scribe-style note | A written record of a bridge or meeting. | Covers the pain that bridge notes stay in people’s heads. |
| Solution architect | The tribe architect the form says to consult before the ideation meeting. | Their recommendation is a blank line until they write it. |
| Enterprise architect | The second architect the form names, if the tribe one is not the right reviewer. | Same blank line. |
| Group AI Use Case Library | AXA’s shared list of AI use cases other entities have already used. | Leave the Yes box unchecked until that list is searched. |
| Dynatrace | The application and user-experience monitor. Models already sit inside detection. | Record it as an existing AXA Japan solution. |
| Splunk | The log search tool. | Same. It stays the log source. |
| Silva | The ticket platform drawn with ServiceNow on the 24x7 diagram. | The value box leaves correlation there. |
| War room | Technology SREs and Application Support working the incident together. | They are the users of the Teams assist. |
