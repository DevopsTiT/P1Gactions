The lines below are still filled, because the form asks for the data type, the boundary, and samples.

The output is text in the Microsoft Teams war-room channel.
A person asked about one open PagerDuty incident, or asked one question.
The reply is a catch-up, a status draft, or a bridge note.
Insights, only if that agent is enabled, returns one sentence about past incidents the user can see.
Shift returns nothing in this pilot.

Customer date of birth, My Number, financial fields, and health fields are not designed into this output.

BOUNDARY OF THE OUTPUT

[ ] Fixed structure
    A fixed structure is the same fields every time, such as a name and an address pulled out of a paragraph.
    This reply has headings a reviewer can look for, and the sentences under those headings are written for this incident.

[X] Indeterminant specific structure
    The expected shape is one of these:
    - SRE catch-up: what the incident says, then the starter checks from the linked runbook.
    - Status draft: current state, what is being checked, and when the next update is. A person sends it.
    - Scribe note: time, what was said, and the next check. The kept result is text.
    - Insights, only if enabled: one sentence that answers the question from incidents the user can see.
    The wording is generated. It stays on that one incident or that one question.
    The form’s example of this boundary is RAG. Here the grounding text is the PagerDuty incident, the linked runbook, the Teams question, and, for Scribe, the bridge audio.

[ ] Open content
    Open content is a new article or a blog post, with no expected shape.
    This pilot asks for a briefing, not an article.
