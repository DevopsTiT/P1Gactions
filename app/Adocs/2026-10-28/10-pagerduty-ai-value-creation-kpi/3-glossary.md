# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care on this slide |
|---|---|---|
| Adoption KPI | How much work the assist actually touches. | Here it is a count of incidents, not premium or documents. |
| Clock A | Minutes to write the catch-up by hand until a reviewer accepts it. | The baseline. It does not exist yet. |
| Clock B | Minutes from the Teams question until the reviewer accepts the AI reply. | The assisted time. |
| Minutes saved | Clock A minus Clock B. | The main KPI. Median of the five incidents. |
| Median | The middle value when the five results are sorted. | One slow incident does not set the whole KPI. |
| Time study | Two people or one reviewer timing the same kind of task. | The measurement method to tick. |
| Reviewer sheet | A short log of start, stop, and usable yes or no. | Where Clock A is recorded. |
| FTE | Full-time equivalent, a headcount translated from hours. | Do not fill it until minutes and volume are both real. |
| Test service | A PagerDuty service that alerts only the pilot. | Where the five clocks run. |
