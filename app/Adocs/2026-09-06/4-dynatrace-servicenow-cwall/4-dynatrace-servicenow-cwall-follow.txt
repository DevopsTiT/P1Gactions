Dynatrace + ServiceNow (+ “CWall”)

CWall: not an official product name. Likely means CMDB, a NOC wallboard, or internal code.

Dynatrace = detect problems + Davis root cause + topology
ServiceNow = incidents, changes, CMDB (inventory of CIs)

Typical flow:
1. Dynatrace Problem opens
2. Notification creates/updates ServiceNow Incident
3. Incident links to CMDB CI (if sync works)
4. Fix → Problem closes → ticket updates

Three lanes:
- Incident integration (problems → tickets)
- Event Management (events → em_event)
- CMDB / Service Graph (hosts/services → CIs)

SRE care: correct CI + less ticket noise + RCA link in the ticket.
