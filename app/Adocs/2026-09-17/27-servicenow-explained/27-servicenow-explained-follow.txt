ServiceNow explained (SRE beginner)

What it is:
  Ticket / IT process platform. Official record of the outage work.

Vs other tools:
  Dynatrace = detect Problem
  ServiceNow = INC ticket
  PagerDuty = page human
  Splunk = search logs

ITSM vs ITOM:
  ITSM = Incidents (tickets) — your Connector creates these
  ITOM = events — classic notification can keep this ON
  Keep classic ITSM OFF to avoid two INCs

Key INC fields:
  number (INC0017788), sys_id, short_description,
  impact/urgency, assignment_group, correlation_id, work notes

Your Dynatrace pack:
  OPEN:  snow-create-incident → comment
  CLOSE: snow-search → snow-resolve
  API:   /api/now/v2/table/incident

Instance example: https://silvastg.service-now.com
