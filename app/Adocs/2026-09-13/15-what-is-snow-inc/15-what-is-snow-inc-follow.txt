What is ServiceNow INC?

INC = Incident.
It is a ServiceNow ticket for something that went wrong (unplanned issue).
Number looks like INC0012345.

In our workflow:
Dynatrace Problem opens → create ServiceNow INC
correlation_id on the INC = Dynatrace Problem ID

Not the same as:
- Dynatrace "Problem" (monitoring object)
- ServiceNow PRB / Change / Request (other ticket types)
