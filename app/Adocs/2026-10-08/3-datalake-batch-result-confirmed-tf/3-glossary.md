# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care |
|---|---|---|
| Today (Splunk range) | From midnight to now | Shows what the 08:00 email contains |
| `log.source` | Log file path in Dynatrace | Replaces the Splunk index |
| Records detector | Opens a problem when rows are returned | One problem per night here |
| UDM | Downstream data platform | "sent to UDM successfully" means delivery worked |
