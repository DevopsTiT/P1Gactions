# Glossary

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Generative | The assistant writes the sentences. |
| Discriminative | A score, a yes or no, or a category. That is the other data type on this form. |
| Field or domain specific structure | The reader expects a catch-up, a verify-list, a status draft, or a bridge note. The wording changes with the incident. The form’s example is RAG. |
| Fixed structure | The same fields every time, such as a name and an address taken from a paragraph. |
| Open content | A new article with no expected shape. |
| Catch me up | The request for the story of one open incident. |
| Angles to verify | Possible checks. They are not a confirmed cause. |
| Scribe wrap-up | The bridge audio turned into a text timeline. |
| Primary | The on-call employee who asks, checks, and sends. |
