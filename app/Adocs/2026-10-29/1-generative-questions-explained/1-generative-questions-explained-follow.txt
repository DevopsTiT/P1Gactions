Generative questions on the Output slide

These are the questions Primary types after @PagerDuty. The assistant writes text. That is why the slide ticks Generative and Text.

INC-48291, Dynatrace P-88421, and INC-47102 are the example story on the slide. They are not live tickets.

1. Catch me up on INC-48291
Someone just joined, or just got the page, and wants the story in one note.
The sample answer says: ebroker-api, P2, acknowledged by Primary at 16:05 JST, source Dynatrace P-88421, related past incident INC-47102.
Starter checks: agrex and zipcode dependency, a recent deploy or config change, and whether the log line is "Address conversion failed…" or a broad 5xx.
Primary still opens Dynatrace and compares the note.

2. What are some likely root causes?
This asks for angles to verify. It does not close the incident.
The sample angles are:
- agrex zipcode dependency latency, 5xx, or timeout
- unusual zip input volume
- ebroker address-service config or URL change after deploy
- network path to the intraxa host
Causes stay a draft. Primary replaces that draft with confirmed evidence before the PIR is published.

3. Draft an internal status update for INC-48291
This asks for stakeholder wording: title, impact, start time, status, evidence so far, next update, owner.
The sample status is Investigating. Next update is 30 minutes. Owner is Primary.
A person sends it. The tool only drafts it.

4. Wrap me up, including Scribe notes for INC-48291
The bridge audio is the input. The output is a timed text note.
16:08 bridge opened. 16:10 upstream timed out. 16:12 next check is the last deploy. 16:14 finish those two checks before the next status.
A spoken employee name can appear in that note.

5. Which service has the most P2 incidents in the last 14 days?
This is the Insights question. The sample answer is one sentence: among the services this user can see, ebroker-api has the most P2 incidents.
Ask it only if Insights is enabled.

Where they sit on the form
They match "field or domain specific structure." The reader expects a catch-up, a verify-list, a status draft, a bridge note, or one history sentence. The sentences are generated from the incident, the runbook, the question, and, for Scribe, the bridge audio.
They are not a fixed name-and-address extract, and they are not an open blog post.
