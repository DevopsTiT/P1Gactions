# Glossary

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| RAG | Retrieval-augmented generation. Retrieve the source, then write the answer from it. |
| Retrieval | The step that fetches the incident, the runbook, or the bridge audio. |
| Generation | The step that writes the sentences. |
| Field or domain specific structure | The form’s name for a note with an expected shape. RAG is their example. |
| Grounding text | The material the sentences are based on. Here it is the incident and the runbook. |
| Draft cause | An angle to verify. Primary replaces it with confirmed evidence before the PIR is published. |
