What RAG means on this slide

RAG means retrieval-augmented generation.

Two steps:
1. Retrieve. Get the material that belongs to this question.
2. Generate. Write the sentences from that material.

The form uses RAG as the example of "field or domain specific structure." The reader expects a catch-up, a list of angles to verify, a status draft, or a bridge note. The sentences change with the incident.

For these war-room questions, the retrieved material is:
- the open PagerDuty incident
- the runbook linked on that service
- the question Primary typed in Teams
- for Scribe, the bridge audio, with a text note as the result

The full Splunk or Dynatrace store is not connected.

Catch me up retrieves the incident and writes the story plus starter checks.
Likely root causes retrieves the same incident and writes angles to verify. The cause stays a draft until Primary confirms it.
Draft a status writes stakeholder wording. A person sends it.
Wrap me up turns the bridge into a timed note.
The 14-day P2 question is Insights, only if that agent is enabled. It retrieves incidents this user can see and writes one sentence.

Primary still checks the note before acknowledge, escalate, resolve, or PIR publish.
