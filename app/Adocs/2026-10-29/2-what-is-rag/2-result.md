# Result

Say this in the meeting: RAG means the note is written from retrieved material, not from a blank page. For this pilot that material is the open incident, the linked runbook, and the Teams question.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Retrieve | Pull the incident, the runbook, and the question. |
| Generate | Write the catch-up, the draft status, or the bridge note. |
| Check | Primary compares it with the detector before acting. |
