# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Session | One logged-in device or browser using your account. |
| Revoke | Sign that session out so it must log in again. |
| SSO | Single sign-on: logging in with your company account. |
| Identity provider | The system behind SSO, such as Okta or Entra ID. |
| 2FA | Two-factor authentication: a second code on top of the password. |
