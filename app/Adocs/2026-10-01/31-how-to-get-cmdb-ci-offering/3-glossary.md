# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Technology Management Service | A technical service (platform or OS team). It has no Service Offering. |
| Business service | A service the business uses. It has offerings per environment. |
| Service Offering | The environment-level child of a business service. Stored in incident cmdb_ci. |
| Incident history fallback | Reusing the offering humans chose on earlier incidents for the same host CI. |
| offering_history | New task 2 output listing those earlier incidents. |
