# Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| cmdb_rel_ci | SILVA table of relationships between CIs, e.g. a host Depends on a service. |
| Depends on | Relationship type: the child CI is used by the parent service. |
| ci_services | New task 2 output listing every service linked to the host CI. |
| Service Offering | The environment-level child of a business service. Mandatory on the SILVA form. |
| SERVICE_MAP | Manual map in task 2 to force a business service by app code, trigram or service tag. |
