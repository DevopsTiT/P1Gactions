# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance window | Dynatrace period where alerts are expected | Real reason to skip tickets |
| AGO_Maintenance tag | Entity tag set by the AGO team | May be a static label, not a window |
| USE_MAINTENANCE_TAG | New task 1 setting | Decides if the tag blocks tickets |
| offering_candidates | All offerings of the business service | Shows if an environment-matching offering exists |
