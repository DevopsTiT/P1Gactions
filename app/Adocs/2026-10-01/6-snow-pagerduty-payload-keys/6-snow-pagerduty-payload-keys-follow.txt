SNOW AND PAGERDUTY PAYLOAD KEYS

SNOW CREATE (POST /api/now/v2/table/incident) - 16 keys
caller_id, u_on_behalf_of, contact_type, company, u_environment, business_service, service_offering,
cmdb_ci (only if found), category, subcategory, impact, urgency, assignment_group,
short_description, description, correlation_id
Reference (need sys_id): caller_id, u_on_behalf_of, company, business_service, service_offering, cmdb_ci, assignment_group

SNOW RESOLVE (PATCH /api/now/v2/table/incident/<sys_id>) - 5 keys
state (6), close_code, close_notes, comments, work_notes

PAGERDUTY TRIGGER (POST https://events.pagerduty.com/v2/enqueue)
routing_key, event_action=trigger, dedup_key=dt-problem-<display_id>, client, client_url,
payload: summary, source, severity, group, component, custom_details
custom_details: problem_id, event_name, service_name, host, environment, business_service,
  assignment_group, db_type, trigram, snow_correlation_id, snow_incident, snow_incident_url
links: [{href, text}]

PAGERDUTY RESOLVE
routing_key, event_action=resolve, dedup_key (same as trigger)
