# Glossary

| Term | What it means | Why you care |
|---|---|---|
| u_business_service | SILVA field for the business service | Payload key for the service |
| cmdb_ci | Shown as "Service Offering" in SILVA | Payload key for the offering |
| u_configuration_item | SILVA field for the host or component | Payload key for the host |
| Bracketed paste | Terminal adds `[200~` around pasted text | Breaks the command; turn it off with bind |
